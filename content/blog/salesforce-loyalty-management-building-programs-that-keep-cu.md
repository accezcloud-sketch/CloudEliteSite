## 1. Designing Your Loyalty Program

### The Problem
Many businesses struggle to move beyond basic discount-based loyalty programs that fail to build genuine emotional connections with customers. This often results in programs that are perceived as transactional rather than valuable, leading to low engagement and limited impact on customer lifetime value.

### Why It Happens
This stems from a lack of deep customer understanding and the absence of flexible program design tools. Without the ability to segment customers, offer tiered rewards, or create engaging experiences beyond simple point accumulation, programs become stagnant and uninspiring. In the dynamic Saudi market, where consumers are increasingly digitally savvy and demand personalized interactions, generic loyalty schemes quickly fall by the wayside.

### What a Consultant Does
A seasoned Salesforce consultant will work with your team to define clear loyalty program objectives aligned with your overall business strategy. This involves conducting a thorough analysis of your target audience, understanding their purchasing habits and preferences, and identifying key drivers of loyalty. We then leverage Salesforce Loyalty Management's capabilities to design a multi-tiered program with flexible rules for earning and redeeming rewards, incorporating elements like experiential rewards, partner integrations, and personalized offers tailored to the Saudi consumer. This ensures your program is not just a discount scheme, but a comprehensive engagement strategy.
